# Hardy-Weinberg census and a cell dilution

A population of **2000** diploid individuals mates at random and satisfies the Hardy-Weinberg assumptions (very large effective size, no selection, no mutation, no migration, discrete generations, equal allele frequencies in the sexes).

At one autosomal locus with two alleles **B** and **b**, the frequency of allele **B** is **p = 0.35** exactly and the frequency of allele **b** is **q = 0.65** exactly (p + q = 1).

1. Under Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, the expected genotype frequencies are `p^2` (BB), `2pq` (Bb), and `q^2` (bb). Compute `n_het`, the expected number of heterozygous (Bb) individuals in this population. This is an exact integer; do not round.

Separately, a laboratory flask holds **6.00 mL** of a uniform cell suspension at a concentration of **800000 cells/mL**. A researcher transfers **0.50 mL** of this suspension into a tube containing **4.50 mL** of fresh medium (final volume **5.00 mL**), mixing uniformly. Assume no cells are created or destroyed during the transfer and the suspension is perfectly uniform.

2. Compute `conc`, the cell concentration of the diluted tube in cells/mL. This is an exact integer; do not round.

Both answers are integers. Report them with no decimal point, no units, and no thousands separators.

End your response with a final line of exactly this form and nothing after it:

FINAL: n_het=<integer> conc=<integer>

Example of the required format (values are not the answer): `FINAL: n_het=100 conc=10000`

The final line is the only part graded.
