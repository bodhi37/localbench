# Distance modulus of a star

A star has apparent magnitude **m = 9.520** and absolute magnitude **M = 3.500**.

Use the distance-modulus relation exactly as given:

```
m - M = 5 * log10(d / 10)
```

where **d** is the distance in parsecs (pc), and `log10` is the base-10 logarithm.

Use **log10(2) = 0.3010** exactly for any logarithm you need. (Hint: express your argument as a power of 2 times a power of 10.)

1. Compute `d_pc`, the distance in parsecs. It is an exact integer; report it with no decimal point.

Convert to light-years using **1 pc = 3.262 ly** exactly:

```
d_ly = d_pc * 3.262
```

2. Compute `d_ly`, the distance in light-years, rounded **half-up to exactly 3 decimal places**.

End your response with a final line of exactly this form and nothing after it:

FINAL: d_pc=<integer> d_ly=<number to 3 dp>

Example of the required number format (values are not the answer): `FINAL: d_pc=10 d_ly=32.620`

The final line is the only part graded. Do not include units.
