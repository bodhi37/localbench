# Neutralising a tank of dilute sulfuric acid

A vineyard technician must fully neutralise a tank containing **250.0 mL** of aqueous sulfuric acid at a concentration of **0.400 mol/L**, using solid potassium hydroxide (KOH).

The neutralisation is complete and follows

```
2 KOH + H2SO4 -> K2SO4 + 2 H2O
```

with no side reactions. All of the sulfuric acid reacts, and exactly the
stoichiometric amount of KOH is used.

Molar masses to use (g/mol): KOH = 56.11, H2SO4 = 98.08, K2SO4 = 174.26.

Report two masses:

* `koh_g`: the mass of KOH consumed, in grams;
* `k2so4_g`: the mass of K2SO4 produced, in grams.

Give each mass rounded **half-up to exactly 3 decimal places**.

End your response with a final line of exactly this form and nothing after it:

FINAL: koh_g=<number to 3 dp> k2so4_g=<number to 3 dp>

Example of the required number format (values are not the answer): `FINAL: koh_g=1.234 k2so4_g=5.678`

The final line is the only part graded. Do not include units or thousands separators.
