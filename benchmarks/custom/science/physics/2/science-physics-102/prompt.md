# Ramp and rough floor

A block of mass 2.50 kg is released **from rest** at the top of a straight ramp and slides down it. The ramp is **frictionless**, is inclined at **25.0°** to the horizontal, and its sliding surface is **3.60 m** long measured along the incline.

At the bottom, the block crosses smoothly onto a long horizontal floor (no energy is lost in the transition). The floor is rough with a coefficient of kinetic friction of **0.350** against the block. The block slides along the floor until it stops. The block never leaves the surface. Air resistance is negligible.

Use **g = 9.80 m/s²** exactly.

Report two quantities:

* `v_bottom`: the block's speed at the bottom of the ramp, in m/s;
* `d_stop`: the distance the block travels along the horizontal floor before stopping, in m.

Give each value rounded **half-up to exactly 3 decimal places**.

End your response with a final line of exactly this form and nothing after it:

FINAL: v_bottom=<number to 3 dp> d_stop=<number to 3 dp>

Example of the required number format (values are not the answer): `FINAL: v_bottom=1.234 d_stop=5.678`

The final line is the only part graded. Do not include units.
